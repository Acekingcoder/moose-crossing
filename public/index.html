How to play

  1. Moose? Hit BRAKE as fast as you can.
  2. Beaver, goose, hockey player? Don't brake. −50.
  3. Fastest foot wins. Braking at nothing costs 25.
  4. Lowest score at the end loses. The loser pays the stake (like buying Timmies) and does a forfeit.
